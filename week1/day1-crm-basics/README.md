📌 What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. It is a software system that helps businesses organize customer information, manage communication, monitor sales activities, and provide support services from a single platform.

CRM enables organizations to:

Store customer details in an organized way
Build and maintain strong customer relationships
Monitor sales and business operations
Automate routine business tasks
Improve team collaboration and efficiency
🌟 Why Businesses Prefer Salesforce

📘 Basic Salesforce Concepts
🏢 Account

An Account refers to a company, organization, or institution.

Example: College

👤 Contact

A Contact is an individual associated with an Account.

Example: Student

🎯 Opportunity

An Opportunity represents a possible business process or successful deal.

Example: Student Enrollment Process

🔄 CRM Process Flow

Lead → Contact → Opportunity → Customer

🔹 Lead

A person who shows interest in a service or product.

Example: A student requesting information about admissions.

🔹 Contact

A confirmed individual whose information is officially stored for communication.

Example: A student who completed and submitted the application form.

🔹 Opportunity

An ongoing process with the possibility of success.

Example: Admission or course enrollment process.

🔹 Customer

The final successful result after completing the process.

Example: A student successfully admitted into the college.
